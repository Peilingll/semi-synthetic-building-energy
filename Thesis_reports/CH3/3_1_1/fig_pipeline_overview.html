Fig. 3.1 — End-to-end methodological workflow. The figure presents the four processing stages and shows how they correspond to the three research objectives. The first stage pairs street-view images with the target buildings and retains information on image source, view quality, and pairing status. The second stage uses the eligible images to infer building type, construction year, and floor count; predicted values and invalid responses are recorded separately. The third stage converts the inferred building type and construction year into a TABULA-NL archetype cell through a deterministic lookup, providing the associated U-values and preserving their provenance. The final stage assembles the features used by the downstream energy class classifier and prepares the geometric and thermal inputs required to calculate H′tr. The outputs of this pipeline form the basis of the controlled evaluation described in Chapter 4.
