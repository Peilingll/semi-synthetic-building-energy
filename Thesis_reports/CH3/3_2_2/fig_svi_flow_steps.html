Fig. 3.3 — Image transformation from a retained 360° panorama to building-level facade crops. Every panel shows real pipeline output for one panorama–building pair (panorama 686679844161937, OpenFACADES building_id 276637663, BAG pand_id 0599100000611463; Rotterdam, grid cell 0252). (1) Raw equirectangular panorama. (2) Map drawn from 01_data/footprint.geojson and 01_data/aov.csv: the target footprint, the 21 candidate cameras that see it within the 30 m isovist radius, the AoV wedge of the followed panorama and the 4 cameras retained after AoV ranking. (3) GroundingDINO detection as annotated by OpenFACADES (02_img/annotated_img, box from 02_img/building_bbox.csv). (4) The detected region as it appears in the equirectangular image and after perspective reprojection (02_img/individual_building). (5) All crops of the same BAG building in the per-building SVI manifest, ranked by AoV. Imagery © Mapillary contributors (CC BY-SA 4.0).
