Fig. 3.3 — SVI acquisition, image–building pairing, cropping and building-level aggregation. The workflow follows the OpenFACADES processing sequence (Liang et al., 2025), with steps introduced or formalised in this study shown in blue. A BAG residential pre-filter is inserted before image processing; after cropping, a formal building_id → pand_id lookup and building-level image cap produce the SVI manifest. Retained residential footprints and Mapillary panorama metadata jointly enter the 30 m isovist pairing step. Candidate views are screened by AoV, limited to three panoramas per OpenFACADES footprint, downloaded, detected, reprojected, cropped and quality filtered. Crops are then linked to BAG buildings and limited to eight per pand_id. The illustrative image strip shows one panorama–building pair and its resulting facade crop (Rotterdam; panorama 686679844161937, building 276637663; the green box is drawn for illustration); numeric filtering thresholds and step-wise counts are reported in Table 3.2.
