Fig. 3.3 — SVI acquisition, image–building pairing and quality filtering. The workflow is the OpenFACADES framework (Liang et al., 2025) with two local adaptations, shown in blue. First, the published pipeline is split into a footprint-acquisition stage and an image-processing stage so that a BAG residential pre-filter can be inserted between them: OpenFACADES footprints and BAG polygons are reprojected to EPSG:28992 and a footprint is retained only when its centroid falls inside a BAG residential building polygon, so buildings outside the target residential stock never enter image processing. Second, because OpenFACADES keys every crop by its own building_id while all reference attributes and EPC labels in this study are keyed by the BAG pand_id, the same centroid rule is applied again after image processing to build a formal building_id → pand_id lookup table; crops for which no BAG match can be established are excluded. The intermediate stage is applied as published: Mapillary 360° panorama metadata are retrieved for the study extent, isovist analysis with a 30 m search radius establishes candidate panorama–building pairs, candidates are screened by angle of view, retained panoramas are downloaded at 2,048-pixel resolution, GroundingDINO locates the target building, and the detection is reprojected to a perspective view, cropped to a single building and passed through the image quality filter. The numeric thresholds of the AoV and quality filters, and the number of buildings and images surviving each step, are given in Table 3.2.
