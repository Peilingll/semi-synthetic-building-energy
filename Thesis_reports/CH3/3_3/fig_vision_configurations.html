Fig. 3.5 — The three vision inference configurations and the scope of training in each. All three lanes take the same per-building street-view crops and end at the same prediction positions, but they differ in two respects that the configuration table cannot show. The first is the extent of the trained region: ResNet-50 updates the backbone, the shared trunk and the heads; DINOv2 leaves the backbone frozen and trains only the trunk and heads; InternVL3-2B updates nothing and is prompted instead. The second is where the multiple images of one building are combined (red markers): the two trained configurations fuse early, averaging the per-image feature vectors before any prediction is made, whereas the VLM fuses late, producing one answer per image and reducing them by mode and median at building level, with unparsable or out-of-range answers excluded. The right-hand panels show that M3 and M2 share the same trunk and differ only in the head: three attribute heads with the equally weighted composite loss for M3, a single energy classifier for M2, with the 7-class and binary tasks trained separately. For the VLM the same substitution is made in the prompt rather than in a head. “Zero-shot” here means only that no model parameter is trained on the study data: the prompt wording was designed on a fixed, class-balanced 200-building development subset and frozen before hold-out evaluation, so development labels do enter the analysis. Configuration details are listed in Table 3.3.
