# 13.04.2026 — Step 1, Step 2, S3/S4 Random Forest

## What was done

### Step 1: 3D derived features + residential filter
- Filtered `bag_3dbag_ep_joined.parquet` (3,500 buildings) to residential only (gebruiksdoel contains woonfunctie)
- Computed 7 derived columns: volume, envelope_area, shape_factor, building_height, num_floors_estimated, floor_area_estimated, lod2_quality_flag
- Removed 1 building with volume=0/NaN
- Output: `residential_with_3d_features.parquet` (3,496 rows x 8 columns)

### Step 2: TABULA matching
- Extracted NL existing-state Gen subtype from `tabula-calculator.xlsx` into `tabula_nl.csv` (24 rows = 4 types x 6 periods)
- Gebouwtype mapping: Rijwoning tussen/hoek -> TH, Appartement -> AB, Twee-onder-een-kap/Vrijstaande woning -> SFH, Woongebouw -> MFH
- Match rate 99.97% (3,495/3,496), only 1 houseboat unmatched
- Output: `residential_tabula_matched.parquet` (3,495 rows x 16 columns)

### Step 3-4: S3/S4 Random Forest
- S3 features: bouwjaar, building_type, u_wall, u_roof, u_floor, u_window
- S4 features: S3 + volume, envelope_area, shape_factor, building_height, num_floors
- Labels: Energieklasse with A+ and above merged into "A+", 8 classes total
- 5-fold stratified CV, RandomForest(n_estimators=200, class_weight='balanced')

## Findings
- Residential filter only removed 3 buildings (EP-Online Gebouwklasse=W already pre-filtered)
- NL.01 period (pre-1964) accounts for 2,545 buildings (73%), heavily skewed toward old buildings
- S3/S4 perform poorly on F/G labels (precision 0.09-0.16) due to small sample size and identical U-values within the same period
- Adjacent labels (B/C/D/E) show significant confusion — inherent limitation

## Results
- **S3 accuracy: 38.2% +/- 2.4%**
- **S4 accuracy: 46.7% +/- 1.2%**
- **3D marginal contribution: +8.5pp**
- Random baseline (8 classes): 12.5%
- Best predicted: A+/A (precision 0.64-0.81), worst: F/G (0.09-0.16)

## Decisions
- Merged A+/A++/A+++/A++++ into "A+" — too few samples for separate classes
- Placed tabula_nl.csv in data/processed/ (derived artifact, not raw data)
- Used Gen subtype as TABULA representative (U-values identical across subtypes for NL.02+)

### Step 5-6: VLM street-view inference test (added 14.04.2026)

#### What was done
- Stratified sampling by Gebouwtype x tabula_period: 36 buildings, removed 1 with no street view, final 35
- Manually captured Google Maps Street View screenshots, saved as `data/processed/mvp_testing_data/{pand_id}.png`
- Produced `vlm_ground_truth.csv` (35 buildings with pand_id, bouwjaar, Gebouwtype, b3_bouwlagen, Energieklasse, coordinates)
- VLM inference: Gemini 2.5 Flash (29 buildings) + Qwen 3.5 Flash via OpenRouter (6 buildings, Gemini free quota exhausted)
- Prompt requests JSON output: building_type, surface_material, construction_year, num_floors, window_to_wall_ratio
- Per-building comparison against ground truth

#### Results
- **Type accuracy: 80.0%** (28/35 correct) > 60% PASS
- **Year MAE: 29.5 years** (median 16) > 20 FAIL
- **Floor MAE: 0.57** < 1.5 PASS

#### Findings
- Year MAE inflated by a few extreme outliers (mean 29.5 but median only 16), most predictions are reasonable
- Gemini free tier quota: 20 requests/day/model, hit limit mid-run, switched to OpenRouter Qwen 3.5 Flash
- 29 buildings via Gemini, 6 via Qwen — mixed models may introduce bias (acceptable for MVP)
- AB vs TH frequently confused: Dutch old-city apartments visually resemble terraced houses

#### Decisions
- Increased sample to 35 (spec said 20) for better coverage across 4 types x 6 periods
- Year MAE median (16 years) better represents typical prediction quality; mean skewed by outliers
