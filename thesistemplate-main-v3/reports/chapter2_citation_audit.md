## Scope and review protocol

- **Source reviewed:** `content/02_literature_review.tex`
- **Order:** citations are reviewed in the order of their first appearance in Chapter 2.
- **Unit of review:** every claim attached to a citation is checked against the cited paper, rather than checking only whether the paper concerns the same general topic.
- **Evidence standard:** the table records the paper section, page, table, or figure where possible, followed by a faithful paraphrase of the supporting evidence. An abstract alone is not treated as evidence for a detailed methodological or numerical claim.
- **Verdicts:** **Supported** means the source directly supports the complete claim; **Partially supported** means only part of the claim is supported or the wording is broader than the evidence; **Contradicted** means the source conflicts with the claim or the bibliographic record points to a different work; **Unresolved** means the necessary full text or source location is unavailable.
- **Editing rule:** this audit does not silently rewrite Chapter 2. Every wording or citation change required by the evidence is first recorded below.

## Audit summary

All 24 citation keys were reviewed in first-appearance order. Nineteen keys had matching full text available through Zotero. Four keys lacked a matching local item or attachment (`rvo2011`, `iso13789`, `zirak2020`, and `oquab2023`); authoritative external material was used where available, but `zirak2020` remains unresolved because the primary full text could not be inspected. One key (`dalach2025`) points to a bibliographic record whose title matches a 2026 preprint, while its year and first-author name are incorrect and the matching paper is absent from Zotero.

### Verdict distribution

- **Supported without a substantive claim correction:** 13 keys (`shen_archetype_2024`, `loga2016`, `wurm2021`, `dochev2020`, `milojevicdupont2023`, `garbasevschi2021`, `zeng2024`, `oquab2023`, `mayer2023uk`, `mayer2023dk`, `liu2025`, `sun2026`, and `hettinga2023`). Two of these, `liu2025` and `sun2026`, still require author-name corrections in the bibliography.
- **Supported with an explicit qualification or minor wording correction:** 3 keys (`bass2022`, `dabrock2025`, and `liang2025`).
- **Partially supported:** 5 keys (`reinhart2016`, `iso13789`, `he2016`, `zhu2025`, and `pan_nd`).
- **Contradicted as currently recorded or worded:** 2 keys (`rvo2011` and `dalach2025`). The substantive claim attached to `dalach2025` is supported by the matching 2026 preprint, but the current bibliographic record is incorrect.
- **Unresolved:** 1 key (`zirak2020`).

### Required corrections before Chapter 2 is treated as citation robust

1. Resolve the Dutch TABULA classification claim. The current four-size-class and six-period statement is not supported by the cited 2011 RVO source. State the official structure accurately or document the study-specific mapping and its period boundaries.
2. Attach the full ISO 13789 standard and the Zirak paper to Zotero. The detailed heat-transfer formula and the primary Zirak claim cannot otherwise receive a full-text verdict.
3. Add the DINOv2 paper to Zotero so the locally reproducible evidence trail covers `oquab2023`.
4. Narrow the OpenFACADES label description because its metadata are not obtained exclusively from OpenStreetMap.
5. Separate cited model capabilities from study-specific configurations for ResNet-50 and InternVL3. He supports the architecture, while full target-task fine tuning is this study’s configuration. Zhu supports InternVL3’s multimodal capability, while using it without further updates for building attribute estimation is this study’s configuration.
6. Correct Section 2.3 and its synthesis for `pan_nd`. The evaluated target is an EPC-derived facade component rating, principally the wall rating, rather than the registered whole-building EPC band used in the other direct-prediction studies.
7. Correct bibliography metadata: Minghao Liu for `liu2025`; Ce Hou and Qiaosi Li for `sun2026`; and Agata Dalach, year 2026, plus DOI `10.2139/ssrn.6361640` for the matching BEG preprint.
8. Preserve the qualification around Hettinga. Its TABULA and random-forest results use different evaluation populations and class ranges, so they should not be presented as a controlled method comparison.

### Overall robustness conclusion

The core literature argument is supportable, but Chapter 2 is not yet citation robust in its current wording. The highest-risk issues are the unsupported Dutch TABULA matrix description, the misclassification of Pan as whole-building EPC prediction, the separation between model-source claims and thesis-specific training configurations, and the incorrect Dalach bibliographic record. Once these items and the missing full-text attachments are resolved, the remaining qualifications mainly concern scope and evaluation design rather than unsupported central claims.
