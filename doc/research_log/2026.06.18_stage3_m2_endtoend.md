# 2026.06.18 — M2 end-to-end vs decomposed M3 (DINOv2)

> **STATUS: SMOKE TEST / PREVIEW — not the paper result.** This M2 uses a frozen
> DINOv2 embedding + LightGBM/LR head, which is NOT aligned with Stage 1's
> neural-head architecture + training recipe (so M2-vs-M3 here confounds the head
> change with the end-to-end-vs-decomposed change). It establishes the direction
> (M2 ≥ M3, M2 ≈ M1) only. The paper uses the **aligned M2** — reuse Stage 1's
> head + training, energy output — see plan `2026.06.18_stage3_m2_aligned.md`.
> Artifacts renamed `M2-DINOv2-frozenprobe_*`.

## Goal

Test whether predicting the energy label DIRECTLY from the street-view image
(end-to-end) recovers signal that the decomposed M3 route (image → type/year/floor
→ TABULA → LightGBM) throws away. Answers "is SVI actually useful for EPC, or only
useless through the archetype bottleneck?"

Plan: `doc_processed/plan/2026.06.18_stage3_m2_endtoend.md`.

## Setup

- Frozen DINOv2 backbone (same as Stage 1 M3-DINOv2), no training. Per-building
  768-d embedding (mean-pool over valid images, deterministic transforms).
  `src/stage3/extract_embeddings.py` (conda stage1-gpu, ~9 min, no OOM).
- M2 head trained on dev (8,068) embeddings → energy label; evaluated on the
  SAME hold-out common set as M3 (n=2,016). `src/stage3/m2_head.py`.
- Two heads: LightGBM (same learner as M1/M3 → clean end-to-end-vs-decomposed
  isolation) and logistic regression (Mayer 2023 style, better on dense embeddings).
- No type/year/floor, no TABULA — pure image → A–G.

## Results (hold-out, n=2,016)

| Route | macro-F1 | κ | acc |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| M1 (GT ceiling) | 0.172 | 0.233 | 0.356 |
| M3-DINOv2 (decomposed) | 0.150 | 0.083 | 0.292 |
| **M2-DINOv2 (LightGBM head)** | 0.176 | 0.208 | 0.354 |
| **M2-DINOv2 (logreg head)** | 0.187 | 0.176 | 0.286 |

Holding the learner fixed (LightGBM) across all three:
M1 0.172/0.233/0.356 ≈ M2 0.176/0.208/0.354 ≫ M3 0.150/0.083/0.292.

## Interpretation (the key Stage 3 finding)

1. **End-to-end vision matches the GT registry ceiling.** With the same LightGBM
   learner, M2 (image embedding → label) ≈ M1 (GT type/year/floor → label) on all
   three metrics — and the logreg head even exceeds M1 on macro-F1 (+0.015). The
   street-view image carries as much energy-label signal as the ground-truth
   registry attributes do.

2. **The decomposition is the bottleneck, not the vision.** M3 (0.150 / κ 0.083)
   is well below both M1 and M2. Squeezing the image through three coarse
   attributes (type/year/floor) + TABULA destroys signal that the raw embedding
   keeps. M2 > M3 by +0.037 macro-F1 and +0.09–0.13 κ. This is the "M2 > M3"
   outcome we pre-committed to: SVI sees energy-relevant cues beyond type/year
   (glazing, PV, façade condition, size) that the archetype route discards.

3. **But all routes plateau at a low absolute ceiling** (~0.17–0.19 macro-F1):
   the true determinants (internal insulation, heating/ventilation systems,
   retrofit state, floor area in m²) are in NEITHER the registry attributes NOR
   visible from the street. So M2 ≈ M1 means "vision is as good as the available
   registry attributes," not "vision solves EPC." The residual gap to a usable
   classifier is the invisible physics (cf. Hettinga 2023: needs area + socio-
   economic features to reach 71%).

## Thesis implications (reframes RQ2 positively)

- **Answers Goal 1 ("extract energy from SVI") positively**: yes — end-to-end,
  the image equals the registry attributes. The right way to use SVI for energy
  is NOT through archetype-attribute extraction.
- **RQ2 sharpened**: the cost of the decomposed vision pipeline is not "vision is
  weak" but "the archetype decomposition is lossy." Deployable recommendation:
  end-to-end vision over the archetype route for the energy-label task.
- **TABULA/archetype route is for archetype attributes, not energy labels** —
  consistent with the U-value-redundancy finding. The archetype pipeline's value
  is interpretability/attribute extraction (RQ1), not downstream label accuracy.
