# 2026.07.13 — LOCO-Amsterdam (RQ3b): cross-city degradation of the SVI→archetype→kWh pipeline

## Context

RQ3b: hold the whole target city out of training and measure how much the
pipeline degrades. Repositioned thesis claims SVI's legitimate role is
**archetype cell assignment**; #3 (pooled joint-cell) showed this holds only
for the dominant old-town cell, making cross-city generalisation a REQUIRED
check rather than nice-to-have. This log records the LOCO-Amsterdam run.

## Setup

- **Split** (`src/stage1/loco_splits.py`): train = all imaged R+U+D buildings
  (2,075; pooled dev+holdout merged — with the target city fully held out the
  in-pool boundary is meaningless and the source pool is scarce); test = all
  imaged Amsterdam (8,011). SHA `bfec437e`. Pool composition table:
  `T5_loco_pool_composition.md` — train is MORE balanced than test
  (AB 65% vs 94%; NL.01 77% vs 92%); cell coverage near-complete (only
  MFH|NL.02 absent, 2 test buildings); Amsterdam majority-cell baseline 0.868.
- **Paradigms**: DINOv2 frozen (5-fold, CV 0.574±0.021) and ResNet-50 ft
  (5-fold, CV 0.522±0.015), both trained on R+U+D → inferred on all Amsterdam;
  InternVL3 zero-shot reused on the Amsterdam ∩ pooled-holdout subset (1,595),
  split-independent. Downstream LightGBM retrained on R+U+D GT.
- Infra: added `--dev-folds/--holdout/--run-tag/--models` overrides across
  train/eval_holdout/joint_cell_eval and stage3 routes/regression; pooled
  defaults unchanged (T4 regression test: byte-identical). Fallback to pooled
  predictions restricted to zero-shot models only (a pooled-trained model has
  seen the test city → would leak).

## Findings

1. **"Fine-tuned degrades most" — confirmed (upstream).** ResNet-50 ft loses
   −0.082 on both type_acc and joint; DINOv2 frozen loses −0.005 / −0.026.
   ResNet overfits the small source pool (train F1 0.98 / val 0.52). The frozen
   self-supervised backbone is the shift-robust paradigm. (Zero-shot VLM
   doesn't "degrade" — it never trained — but its absolute level is far lower.)
2. **The cell-assignment claim does NOT survive LOCO.** Both trained paradigms
   fall below the (raised) majority-cell baseline. The claim "SVI extends
   TABULA" holds within-distribution for the dominant cell but is NOT
   cross-city-general as stated. → thesis must condition the claim.
3. **Root cause isolated to year.** type_acc holds (0.897) but year MAE doubles;
   period errors follow, so the cell is wrong. Year regression memorised the
   source-city age distribution. Suggests the modality-division principle
   should extend: **year may not belong to vision** — pair registry/other
   sources for period, use SVI for type.
4. **Substitution cost vanishes because M1 collapses too.** Under shift GT
   attributes give almost no downstream signal (κ 0.026); the M3−M1 gap → −0.002.
   The bottleneck is the task/pool, not vision — closes the loop with the pooled
   82%-downstream error split and E6/E8 (pool bias + label entropy).
5. **Downstream washout (honesty note).** M3-ResNet50 downstream marginally
   ABOVE M3-DINOv2 despite worse upstream — once everything sits near the M0
   floor, upstream differences stop propagating. Report both κ, don't over-read.
