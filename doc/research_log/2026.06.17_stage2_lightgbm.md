# 2026.06.17 — Stage 2: downstream energy-label sensitivity (LightGBM)

## Goal

Establish the registry-attribute ceiling for downstream `Energieklasse`
(A–G) classification and quantify each attribute's contribution. This is the
Stage 3 M1 ceiling and answers thesis Goal 2 ("how does the TABULA U-value
lookup influence prediction").

Plan: `doc_processed/plan/2026.06.17_stage2_lightgbm.md`.
Design: `doc_processed/spec/stage_123_design.zh-TW.md` §Stage 2.

## Setup

- **Universe**: dev set 8,068 buildings (same as Stage 1), `dev_fold_indices.parquet`
  5-fold OOF. Holdout not used (Stage 2 is CV per design). Keeps M1/M3 comparable.
- **Label**: `Energieklasse`, A+/A++/A+++/A++++ merged into A → 7 ordinal classes.
- **Features**: U-values looked up deterministically from `tabula_nl.csv` on
  (building_type, tabula_period) — same join as `tabula_matcher`. No per-city
  matched parquet needed.
- **Model**: LightGBM, fixed HP (n_est=400, lr=0.05, num_leaves=31, …),
  `class_weight=None` (decision locked — see below). M0 = per-fold majority.
- **Metrics**: macro-F1, quadratic-weighted κ, per-class P/R, bootstrap 95% CI
  (1000, building-level; reuses `src/stage1/evaluate.py::bootstrap_ci`).
- Code: `src/stage2/{features,metrics,train_eval,run_ablation}.py`.

## class_weight decision (None vs balanced, empirical)

Plan picked `None` to protect ordinal κ. Verified on S_full:

| class_weight | macro-F1 | κ | acc |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **None** | 0.180 | **0.187** | **0.349** |
| balanced | 0.189 | 0.159 | 0.207 |

`balanced` buys +0.009 macro-F1 but costs κ (−0.028) and accuracy (−0.14).
Decision **None** confirmed and locked.

## Interpretation (thesis findings)

1. **TABULA U-value lookup is decorative for downstream energy classification**
   (Goal 2 answer, clean). S_lookup ≈ S_min (Δ −0.001) and removing U-values
   from S_full costs exactly 0.000. Cause: U-values are a deterministic function
   of (building_type, tabula_period), and period is a deterministic function of
   bouwjaar — the tree already has that information from type+year. The U-value
   step carries **no incremental downstream signal** beyond the matching keys.

2. **Construction year is the bottleneck attribute.** It is the single most
   important feature downstream (−0.031 macro-F1 when removed, largest of any).
   Combined with Stage 1 (year is hard but tractable — DINOv2 MAE 9.45 /
   period_acc 0.90; VLM period_acc 0.77), this is the Cross-Stage 2 result:
   extraction-difficulty × downstream-importance peaks on year.

3. **The ceiling itself is low** (S_full macro-F1 0.180, κ 0.187, acc only 0.35
   vs majority 0.31). Diagnosed, not a bug: the dev set is dominated by old
   buildings (median bouwjaar 1925, 75% < 1933). Among pre-WWII stock, year
   barely discriminates EP label (modal class C across 1870s–1930s); the strong
   year→label signal lives only in the sparse post-1970 segment. Spearman
   corr(bouwjaar, energy-ordinal) = −0.096. EP label is driven by
   renovation/insulation state, which no registry attribute captures.

4. **Implication for RQ2 (Stage 3)**: because GT features barely beat majority,
   the M3-vs-M1 gap (cost of replacing registry with vision attributes) will be
   structurally small — there is little downstream signal to lose. The headline
   RQ2 number must be read against this low ceiling, not in absolute terms.

## Full-GT ceiling diagnostic (vision-free, not part of official ablation)

To test whether the low ceiling is intrinsic or a property of the old-city SVI
subsample, the same LightGBM ablation was run on the FULL GT (124,784 buildings,
no image restriction). Script: `scripts/stage2_ceiling_diagnostic.py`.

| Set | n | macro-F1 | κ | acc | median bouwjaar |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| dev (SVI, official) | 8,068 | 0.180 | 0.187 | 0.349 | 1925 |
| full GT | 124,784 | 0.209 | **0.377** | 0.440 | 1938 |

- U-value redundancy holds on full GT too (S_min 0.174 → S_lookup 0.173, −0.001) —
  robust, as expected (it is a deterministic function, sample-independent).
- macro-F1 stays modest everywhere (F/G intrinsically hard), but κ doubles and
  accuracy jumps on the full set. The SVI dev subset sits in the hardest regime:
  street-view coverage skews to old city-centre stock where year barely
  discriminates EP label. → low dev-set ceiling is largely a **sampling-bias**
  effect, not "registry attributes are useless for energy".

## Conclusion

1. **TABULA U-value lookup is decorative for the data-driven downstream**
   (robust, cross-sample). S_lookup ≈ S_min and −u_values Δ = 0.000 on both the
   dev subset and full GT. U-values carry no signal beyond the (type, year)
   matching keys, because they are a deterministic lookup from those keys and a
   tree model already has them. This does NOT condemn TABULA's physics-simulation
   role (untested here) — only its use as a classifier feature.
2. **Construction year is the bottleneck attribute** — most important downstream
   (−0.031), and the one Stage 1 must extract well. Drives the Cross-Stage 2 story.
3. **The dev-set ceiling is low (macro-F1 0.18 / κ 0.19) mainly due to old-city
   SVI sampling bias**, not an intrinsic dead-end: the full-population ceiling is
   materially better (κ 0.38 / acc 0.44). Document as a limitation.
4. **For RQ2/Stage 3**: M3-vs-M1 gaps will be read against a low *and explained*
   dev ceiling. The headline is the relative cost of vision-vs-registry, framed
   with the sampling-bias caveat — not absolute label accuracy.
