# 2026.06.18 — M2-ResNet50 (aligned, full fine-tune): backbone robustness

Confirms the M2 finding on a second backbone with its OWN Stage 1 paradigm.

## Setup

- `ResNet50Energy` = full fine-tune ResNet-50 (ImageNet) + trunk + single 7-class
  energy head; packed forward + discriminative lr (backbone 1e-4 / head 5e-4),
  mirroring Stage 1's `ResNet50FT`. Trained end-to-end on energy (NOT reusing the
  Stage 1 attribute-FT weights; fresh from ImageNet, same as M3-ResNet50's source).
- class-weighted CE, AdamW, cosine, AMP, per-epoch empty_cache, 5-fold, best-fold
  hold-out. batch 8 (8GB GPU). Includes train-time augmentation.
- 256.7 min total, no OOM (all 5 folds). Per-fold best val energy macro-F1:
  [0.193, 0.210, 0.199, 0.201, 0.200]; best fold 1.
- Cmd: `m2_aligned.py --model resnet50_energy --all-folds --batch-size 8 --lr 1e-4 --head-lr 5e-4`.

## Result (hold-out, n=2,016)

| Route | macro-F1 | κ | acc |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| M1 (GT ceiling) | 0.172 | 0.233 | 0.356 |
| M3-ResNet50 (decomposed) | 0.149 | 0.091 | 0.282 |
| **M2-ResNet50 (aligned)** | **0.201** | **0.188** | 0.263 |

- M2 − M3: **+0.052 macro-F1, +0.097 κ** — end-to-end beats decomposed.
- M2 − M1: **+0.029 macro-F1, −0.045 κ** — matches the GT ceiling.

## Robustness conclusion

The "end-to-end ≥ decomposed, ≈ GT ceiling" finding now holds across THREE
independent setups:

| M2 variant | macro-F1 | κ | vs M3 | vs M1 |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| DINOv2 aligned (frozen+neural, cached) | 0.213 | 0.236 | ≫ | ≈/> |
| ResNet50 aligned (full fine-tune) | 0.201 | 0.188 | ≫ | ≈ |
| DINOv2 frozen-probe (LightGBM, unweighted, smoke) | 0.187 | 0.176 | ≫ | ≈ |

Two backbones (frozen ViT-SSL and full-FT CNN), each aligned to its own Stage 1
paradigm, plus an unweighted-head variant — all agree. The conclusion is NOT a
DINOv2 / head / class-weighting artefact: **the archetype decomposition is the
binding constraint, not the vision backbone.** End-to-end vision recovers the lost
signal and equals the registry-attribute ceiling, for any of the tested backbones.
